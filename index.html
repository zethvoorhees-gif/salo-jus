About Us

🍹

"Every glass we pour is a promise of freshness, health, and happiness."

A Local Taste You Can Trust

Salo Jus is a homegrown small business based in Karawang, West Java, specialising in fresh fruit juices, blended drinks, chilled fruit bowls, and healthy beverages made from premium natural ingredients.

Since our founding in 2018, we have served the Karawang community with one simple mission: deliver genuine freshness at an honest price. Every single glass is made fresh to order — no preservatives, no artificial colours, and no shortcuts.

We believe that a healthy drink should not be a luxury. That's why we keep our prices affordable without ever compromising on quality or taste.
