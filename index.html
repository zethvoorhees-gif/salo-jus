About Us

Fresh Drinks,
Authentic Flavour

Salo Jus is a local small business specialising in fresh fruit juices, mixed drinks, and chilled fruit bowls made from the finest quality ingredients. Founded in 2018, we are committed to delivering healthy, affordable, and flavourful beverages for every Indonesian family.

Every glass we serve is made fresh to order — no artificial colouring, no preservatives, and no excessive synthetic sweeteners. Just real fruit and genuine freshness.
